Baby Buddy
One-handed, dark-first activity logging · iOS + Android

A warm, friendly client for a self-hosted Baby Buddy server, built for the 3am reality: log a feeding, sleep or diaper in 1–3 thumb taps, in the dark, while holding a baby. Tap anything below — the frames are live and interactive: start the timer, open a log sheet, switch children, toggle night/offline mode in Settings.

Principles
1–3 taps. Smart defaults (now, last-used type/method, alternating breast) mean the common log is one tap to open, one to save.
Thumb zone. Primary actions live in the lower half; Save spans the bottom of every sheet.
Color = recognition. Each activity keeps its color across cards, timeline and timers — readable half-asleep.
Never type a time. One reusable time-entry component with relative chips & anchors replaces wheel pickers.

The reusable time-entry component, in context
Two shapes, one component. Interval events (feeding, sleep, pumping, tummy) offer “Last 15/30/45m”, combinable Ended + Lasted rows, a back-dated live timer, and smart anchors. Point-in-time events (diaper) use one-tap “ago” chips. Every flow shows a plain-language readout and a ±5m nudge — no wheel pickers.
